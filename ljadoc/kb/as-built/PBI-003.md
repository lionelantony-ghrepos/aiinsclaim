## How to extend

- PBI-004: keep using `requireRole()` and `scope.ts` for query-level access; middleware only guards routes.
- New protected routes: add a rule in `src/lib/auth/route-access.ts` and a nav item in `src/lib/nav.ts`.
- Do not reintroduce client-only role gating.
