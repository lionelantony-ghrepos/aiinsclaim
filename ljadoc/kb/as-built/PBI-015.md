- Decision tables evaluated or seeded: BR-RESERVE-001, BR-DOC-001 (IDs only)
- Entitlements / `requireRole()`: workbench actions require adjuster/supervisor/admin; reads open to intake_agent/siu_analyst/admin; writes scoped via `canWriteClaim`
